Home Assistant · ESP32-P4

Native Home Assistant display

The screen of my house, connected to Home Assistant. It runs directly on the device (ESPHome and LVGL), with no browser: Home Assistant sends it the data, the screen never polls.

Weather and warnings, lights, shutters, heating, solar energy, music, calendar, alarm clock, voice assistant with a local wake word. 21 themes, 7 languages, install from the browser.

Vigie

The world's alerts in one place: official weather warnings of 48 countries, earthquakes, cyclones, volcanoes, floods, fires seen by satellite. On a world map, and a "Near me" page for a given place.

Vigie gathers official sources without replacing them: always follow the instructions of your authorities.
