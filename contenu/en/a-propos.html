About

I'm Axel Duviella. I tinker on Sundays: home automation, small screens and alerts, with the help of AI, and I share what I make in case it helps someone.

How these projects are made

I'm not a developer by trade. I describe what I want, test at home and judge by the result; most of the code is written by AIs, which I have work with written rules and tests. Each project says so in its documentation.

These are personal projects, with no promise of support: they run at my home every day, and feedback is welcome.

Where to find me

  • The code of every project: github.com/Axellum;
  • questions and ideas about the Home Assistant display: the project's Discussions;
  • a problem with Vigie: vigie-web issues.
