Home Assistant · ESP32-P4

Native Home Assistant screen

My home's screen, connected to Home Assistant. It runs on the screen itself (ESPHome and LVGL), with no browser: Home Assistant pushes the data, the screen never polls.

Weather and warnings, lights, shutters, climate, solar energy, music, calendar, alarm clock, voice assistant with a local wake word. 21 themes, 7 languages, installed from the browser.

For the M5Stack Tab5, and the Waveshare ESP32-P4 7-inch (experimental, it is my daily screen).

Vigie

The world's alerts in one place: official weather warnings, earthquakes, cyclones, volcanoes, floods, fires seen by satellite. On a world map, with a "Near me" page for a given place.

Vigie gathers official sources (MeteoAlarm, DWD, Environment Canada, NWS, MetService, Vigicrues, the WMO registry…) without replacing them: always follow your authorities' instructions.

The API
